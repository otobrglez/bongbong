# Bushes and reeds — `static/bushes_sheet.png`

Soft cover: six kinds of bush a map can place, generated by
`tools/spritegen/gen_bushes.py`
(`SPRITE_OUT=static python3 tools/spritegen/gen_bushes.py`). Part of the
woodland pass (BB-74); the trees are docs/TREES_SPEC.md.

---

## 1. What a bush is

**Not an obstacle.** A bush is cover a tank drives through, the same as
tall grass, and for the same reason it cannot be an `Obstacle`: the nav
grid and `maplint` treat every obstacle as impassable. A bush cell is a
soft-cover cell in `Game::grass_cells` beside the tall grass, so it
gets everything grass has, with no code of its own:

| | tall grass | a bush |
|---|---|---|
| conceals its cell | `Terrain::conceals` | same |
| a hull presses it down | `grass::tick` (`crush`), recovering over `grass_crush_recover_seconds` | same, squashed to `bush_crush_flatten` |
| a passing hull shoves it | `push`, a lean | `push`, a sideways shift in whole 2 px blocks |
| a blast, a slug, a well | `flatten`, `flatten_along`, `lean_grass` | same |
| a sonic hammer's wave | `pin` | same |
| fire | chars when its cell burns; stops concealing | same |
| spreads fire | never | never |
| the wind | bends it | nothing (§3) |

What differs is the picture: a grass cell scatters several tufts, a
bush cell grows **one sprite** (`grass::bush_tuft`, a `GrassTuft` with
`bush` set). It is rooted in the middle of its cell, a 2 px block above
the bottom edge. `map::world_to_cell` rounds an edge into the next row,
so a root on the edge would leave the bush out of its own cell for every
cell lookup: the fire that chars it, the hammer that pins it, a
replica's burn.

**Reeds and water.** A bed of reeds lines a bank and burns like any other
soft cover. Soft cover never spreads a fire, and water never burns, so a
stream between two reed beds is a firebreak
(`flame_tests::reeds_char_in_a_lit_cell_but_never_carry_fire_across_a_stream`).

## 2. Sheet layout

256 × 192: 8 columns × 6 rows of 32 px cells, drawn at 1:1 with the art's
own 2×2 blocks, like a wall. A bush is the size of a wall tile, smaller
than a tree's 48 px.

| row | kind | map kind |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | `Bush::Bush`, a round leafy shrub | `bush` |
| 1 | `Bush::Berry`, the same with red berries | `berry_bush` |
| 2 | `Bush::Juniper`, low, spiky and dark | `juniper` |
| 3 | `Bush::Fern`, fronds on the forest floor | `fern` |
| 4 | `Bush::Autumn`, gold and brown | `autumn_bush` |
| 5 | `Bush::Reeds`, cattails | `reeds` |

| col | 0–3 | 4–7 (`BUSH_DRY_COL`) |
|---|---|---|
| meaning | four variants, green | the same four, dry |

The variant is hashed from the cell. A desert map draws the dry half,
recoloured onto the sand and wood ramps with the trees' own `DRY_RAMP`
(docs/TREES_SPEC.md §3.1); the autumn bush is already gold and brown,
so only its few green pixels change.

## 3. Drawing

`grass::draw_bush`: upright, mirrored by its hash. A hull holding it down
foreshortens it toward its root, never under a third of its height
(`bush_crush_flatten`). A passing hull shifts it sideways, snapped to the
2 px grid. It does **not** bend in the wind. Rotating a round 32 px
sprite resamples its inside every frame: the churn `obstacle::tree_blit`
was built to avoid, and a bush is stiffer than a blade of grass anyway. A
charred bush is grass's ash stub.

## 4. The builder

The six kinds are tools in the **Plant** group with the nine trees, each
icon on a patch of the theme's floor like tall grass. Plant is a group
of its own because Ground with them was too long a list for a phone. A
sixth group button costs the bar 56 points: a 1280 window drops the BUILD
label and the arena's 1088 window drops the map's name, which the status
line shows instead. Where even a phone's folded palette is too tall
stacked, its groups flow one after another along the rows
(`chrome::Palette`).
