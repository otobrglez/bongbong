# Towers sheet spec

`static/towers_sheet.png`, generated by `tools/spritegen/gen_towers.py`
(no Pillow; `python3 tools/spritegen/gen_towers.py`). The designs are the
ones picked in the design review (docs/defence-towers-prd.md section 12):
T3 capacitor dome (tesla coil), G2 armoured turret (gun tower), B2 sludge
mortar (bio slush).

## Grid

- 8 columns x 6 rows of **48 px cells** (384 x 288), the trees' cell size:
  a tower is drawn centred on its 32 px map cell and overhangs it by 8 px
  on each side, like a tree (`battlefield::tile_half_extent` keeps its
  collider to the cell).
- Designed on a **24 x 24 grid of 2 px blocks**, the density the tanks
  draw at. One design pixel of `#252525` outlines the outside of each
  layer (enclosed gaps stay open).

| row | tower | livery |
|-----|-------|--------|
| 0 | tesla coil | player |
| 1 | tesla coil | enemy |
| 2 | gun tower | player |
| 3 | gun tower | enemy |
| 4 | bio slush | player |
| 5 | bio slush | enemy |

`tower::sheet_row(kind, side) = kind.index() * 2 + (side == Enemy)`.

| col | layer |
|-----|-------|
| 0-3 | the **base**, four standing stages: intact, scuffed, damaged, critical (`tower::stage_of`, the tiles' damage-stage rule) |
| 4 | the **ruin** a dead tower leaves (passable, drawn on the floor) |
| 5 | the **top layer**, intact |
| 6 | the top layer, damaged (stage 2 and 3) |
| 7 | the **glow overlay**: the tesla's lens and two surviving prong tips, the mortar's loaded mouth; empty for the gun |

## Layers

Like a tank's hull and turret, a tower is drawn as separate parts
(`tower::draw_tower`): the base never turns; the top layer is centred on
the same point and **rotated by the tower's heading** for the towers that
turn (gun, bio - `TowerKind::turns`); the glow overlay is drawn over the
top with its charge as alpha, turned with it. The top layer faces **up**
(heading 0), the game's rotation convention.

- **Tesla coil (T3)**: the base is the riveted dome, the side's trim ring
  and three copper prongs (the second snaps off at stage 2). The top is
  the lens dome - it does not turn; it is its own layer so the glow can sit
  over it and the damaged dome can crack independently.
- **Gun tower (G2)**: the base is the steel plate with hazard stripes in
  the side's trim on two corners and the turret ring. The top is the
  turret: twin barrels (muzzle 21 px from the centre, `GUN_MUZZLE_PX`), the
  mantlet and the hatch; the damaged top has one barrel shot short.
- **Bio slush (B2)**: the base is the octagonal plate with trim on two
  corners and three canisters of ooze (the second smashed at stage 2). The
  top is the mortar tube, mouth 12 px from the centre (`BIO_MUZZLE_PX`
  leads the glob a pixel past it).

Anti-air (docs/fpv-swarm.md "Air defence by the towers") needs nothing on
the sheet: the gun tower's top turns on a drone as on a tank, and the
tesla's arc at a drone is the bolt it draws at a tank, ending at the drone
as it is drawn - lifted by its height over its shadow.

## Palette

The Puny palette with its extended steel greys (`PUNY_PALETTE_ALL`), plus
two admissions `tools/check_sheets.py` grants this sheet alone: the player
trim in `TEAM_P1` and the ooze in `punypalette.OOZE` (acid lime, the O1
pick - off the palette on purpose, a glowing liquid that must not read as
grass). The enemy trim is the red ramp. The sheet sits on the grass, so it
is held to the no-green rule; the ooze is not one of the grass greens.

Wear (stages 1-3) is hashed per design pixel: dents one or two palette
steps darker, holes, and at stage 3 char patches. Outlines and lit parts
never wear.

## The pickup

The tower pack is a supply crate with a spanner on its lid
(`static/crates_sheet.png`, written by `tools/spritegen/gen_crates.py`), see
docs/CRATES_SPEC.md.
